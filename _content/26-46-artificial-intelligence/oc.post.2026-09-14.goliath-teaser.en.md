Everyone knows that there's a lot of buzz around the topic of AI at the moment.
And many also have seen headlines and discussions about a certain subtopic: AGI,
Artificial General Intelligence. Or the god mode of AI.

Whenever a new release - especially a major release - comes out, the claim that
this is AGI is in the room. And not only that, it is used as a marketing tool.

A statement like "This is so powerful, we can't release it yet. We have to tame
it." is nothing else than creating a hype and at the same time creating
scarcity. A move from the marketing rulebook.

No one knows how far AGI is away. And no one knows if it will be dangerous or
beneficial for humanity.

Some years ago (before the first big AI wave even started with the ChatGPT
hype), I created a short story around that topic - not Aliens vs Cowboys, but
Aliens vs AGI. Enjoy!

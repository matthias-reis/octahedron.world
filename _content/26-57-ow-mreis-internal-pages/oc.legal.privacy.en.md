# In short

octahedron.world is a personal website with stories, essays and pictures. It
only processes the data it needs to run, plus an anonymous visitor count.

- No ads, no third-party tracking or analytics services
- No embedded content or social media plugins from other providers
- No contact form, no newsletter, no user accounts
- All fonts are served from our own server
- A single cookie remembers the language you picked

only on our behalf and on our instructions; a data processing agreement under
Art. 28 GDPR is in place.

# Server log files

On every request, our server automatically stores:

- the IP address of your device
- date and time of the request
- the requested address and HTTP method
- the status code and the amount of data transferred
- the protocol and encryption used (TLS version)

Your browser, operating system and the page you came from (referrer) are not
stored. We need the log files to run the website securely and reliably and to
detect attacks. Once a day we count how often each page was requested with which
status code; that count contains no IP addresses. The log files themselves are
deleted after 15 days at the latest.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running the website securely (Art.
6(1)(f) GDPR).

# Visitor statistics with Umami

To see which pages are read, we use Umami. Umami runs on our own server at
Hetzner; no data is passed on to third parties.

Umami sets no cookies and stores no IP addresses. It records the page visited,
the page you came from, browser, operating system, device type, screen size,
language and the country the request comes from. To count visits, Umami derives
an anonymous identifier from your IP address and browser details whose basis
changes regularly, so you cannot be recognised over a longer period. It cannot
be traced back to you as a person.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a data-minimal analysis of how our
website is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

# Cookies and browser storage

octahedron.world sets a single cookie, `locale`. It is only created when you
switch the language, stores nothing but that choice and expires after one year.

The interactive puzzle stories of the DICA Club keep your progress in your
browser's local storage. That information never leaves your device, and you can
delete it at any time in your browser settings.

Both are strictly necessary for a feature you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

# Contact by email

If you send us an email, we process your address and the content of your message
to answer it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We delete it once your request is settled,
unless statutory retention periods apply.

# Links to other websites

Links to other websites, such as YouTube or Bluesky, are plain links. Only when
you follow one does that provider's privacy policy apply.

# Encryption

The website is only served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

# Your rights

You have the right to access the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR), to have
it corrected (Art. 16), erased (Art. 17), its processing restricted (Art. 18)
and to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20). You can object to processing
based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21). An email to
[mr@octahedron.world](mailto:mr@octahedron.world) is enough.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority
(Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the Hamburg Commissioner for
Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Der Hamburgische Beauftragte für
Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).
